# Employee Leave Policy
Document ID: DOC-001
Classification: Public to all employees
Owner: Human Resources
Last updated: 2026-09-01

## Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 20 days of annual paid leave per calendar year.

## Sick leave
Employees may take up to 10 days of sick leave per calendar year, subject to the company's reporting process.

## Requesting leave
Submit leave requests through the HR portal at least five working days before planned annual leave when possible. A manager must approve the request.
